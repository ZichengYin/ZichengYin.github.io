## Project Introduction

*Neoclassical Orchestra* is a recording and mixing project for a neoclassical ensemble. The mixing approach centered on three core objectives: achieving dynamic balance across the ensemble, enhancing spatial depth through ambience, and ensuring transparent loudness control.

The project addressed specific tonal issues by applying corrective EQ to the drum tracks to reduce muddiness, while a large-format reverb was added to the overall mix to enhance the sense of space and depth. A bus compressor and L316 limiter were applied on the master bus to control peaks and prevent overload, ensuring a polished and professionally competitive loudness level.

## Project Highlights

- **Dynamic Balance**: Careful level balancing across all orchestral sections to achieve a cohesive and natural ensemble sound.
- **Tonal Correction**: Targeted EQ on drum tracks to reduce low-frequency muddiness and improve clarity.
- **Spatial Enhancement**: Application of a large reverb to the overall mix to create a immersive sense of space and depth.
- **Loudness Management**: Bus compression and L316 limiter on the master bus to control peaks and prevent overload.

## Project Achievements

- Achieved a natural and balanced mix with improved clarity and spatial depth
- Delivered a polished master with competitive loudness levels without sacrificing dynamics
