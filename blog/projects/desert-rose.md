## Project Introduction

*Desert Rose* is a music timing training game that transforms abstract note duration relationships into a more intuitive gameplay experience. Inspired by games such as *Suika Game*, players can combine eighth notes to form quarter notes, and quarter notes to form half notes, thereby understanding the relationships between different durations. Each successful combination triggers corresponding sound effects, providing dual visual and auditory feedback. At the same time, the length of the combination sound effect is positively correlated with the corresponding note duration, manifested as gunfire of varying lengths. The stark contrast between the gunfire and the rose UI conveys a wish for world peace.

## Tech Stack

- **Development Tools**: Unity, Cubase
- **Art Design**: Original note assets, war-themed UI design
- **Sound Design**: Vocoded synthetic sounds (v1.0); gunfire and explosion sounds recorded from games and real objects (v2.0)

## My Role

- Independent development and design
- Game logic programming, including collision detection and combination logic
- Art design and production
- Sound effect recording and editing
- Theme concept and narrative design

## Project Highlights

- **v1.0**: Used candy-colored visuals and vocoded sounds to validate the feasibility of "sound-image-operation" triple feedback for timing training.
- **v2.0**: Adopted a war and peace theme, transforming the visual from ruins to a dawn sky with rose elements; sound effects changed from vocals to gunfire and cannon sounds.
- **Original Art Design**: All game art is original, using rose imagery to symbolize vitality amidst gunfire.
- **Experimental Sound Design**: Gunfire sounds were sourced from game recordings, balloon pops, book slaps, and other self-recorded materials, with precise timing control edited in Cubase.
- **Emotional Narrative**: Uses the peace-evoking piano piece *Nuvole Bianche* as background music to emphasize the theme.

## Project Achievements and Future Plans

- **Current Achievement**: Completed two versions (candy theme and war theme). v2.0 features a complete redesign of visuals and sound system.
- **Future Plans**:
  1. Introduce dynamic difficulty, adjusting note falling speed based on player error rate
  2. Expand with more question types and note combinations
  3. Further explore the relationship between sound intensity and duration perception training
